## 1. Executive result

The pilot found a real statutory transition that demonstrates why a present-day college directory is not enough for historical credential triage.

- Punjab Act No. 5 of 2015 established Maharaja Ranjit Singh State Technical University (MRSPTU). The Act identified eleven districts in its territorial scope and provided that technical colleges in that area would become associated with MRSPTU from a date to be notified by the State Government.
- An MRSPTU university brochure states that the technical institutions in those eleven districts were affiliated to MRSPTU with effect from 1 July 2015. This is a university-published retrospective account, not the primary State Government implementation notification.
- Punjab Act No. 4 of 2018, published on 17 January 2018, created a one-time choice for eligible colleges in the relevant territory to affiliate with either IKGPTU or MRSPTU. It also included a separate option for students admitted in academic session 2015–16 to continue at their existing college and receive their award from IKGPTU under the statutory provision.
- IKGPTU Registrar communications in January and April 2018 describe the application process and documents expected from colleges exercising the choice.
- IKGPTU's 2018 affiliated-college list and 2019 course lists provide useful dated university-published snapshots, but they do not prove a specific college's 2018 branch approval or an individual student's award.

**Research conclusion:** a college's current parent university, historical affiliation, programme approval, campus/location permission and a student's awarding university are distinct claims. A temporal model must represent those claims separately and attach each to the correct source, date range, entity and cohort.

This is not a finding about any real college or graduate.

### 2.1 Legal interpretation safeguard

The 2015 Act and the 2018 amendment must be treated as separate legal versions.

The 2015 Act establishes MRSPTU and a territorial affiliation mechanism, but leaves the effective date of association to a State Government notification. Its transition provision addresses students already pursuing a PTU course before the relevant date.

The 2018 amendment later gives eligible colleges a one-time choice between IKGPTU and MRSPTU. It separately provides an award route for students admitted during session 2015–16.

Consequently, these facts can coexist:

- A college falls within MRSPTU's statutory territory.
- The college becomes associated with MRSPTU under the 2015 transition.
- The college later exercises the one-time statutory choice and affiliates with IKGPTU.
- A student from a protected 2015–16 cohort may have an award route that cannot be inferred from the college's later affiliation.

Do not infer an individual's awarding university from the college's current affiliation field.

### 3.1 Source access notes

- The source URLs in the CSV point to Government of Punjab, India Code, AICTE and IKGPTU/MRSPTU university domains.
- PDF text extraction was available for the reviewed material, but the PDF screenshot endpoint returned cache-miss errors for several files. Those documents were not visually inspected in this pass.
- PB-004 is a poor scan with a date-transcription uncertainty.
- PB-012 appears in IKGPTU's official admissions index, but its linked PDF could not be fetched. This is an access gap, not evidence that the course list did not exist.
- Different documents hosted by the same university are not automatically independent confirmations. Source lineage and document-level scope must be retained.

## 5. Trust Gatekeeper demonstration test

This is a **research test fixture based on a real statutory transition**, not a real student case.

### Hypothetical intake

- Institution type: technical college in one of the eleven districts listed in MRSPTU Act section 5.
- Claimed study period: academic years 2014–15 through 2017–18.
- Credential: B.Tech degree.
- Submitted document: scanned degree with unverified institution, branch, dates and awarding-university claims.

### Expected processing

1. Extract the institution name, campus/address, programme/branch, admission cohort, completion year and named awarding university. Every extracted field remains an unverified document claim.
2. Resolve name aliases so PTU and IKGPTU are not incorrectly treated as separate entities after the statutory name change.
3. Check whether the college falls within MRSPTU's statutory territory. This is a geographic/statutory scope check, not proof of its actual affiliation.
4. Retrieve the State Government notification that implemented the MRSPTU association date. Until captured, mark the date as a university-reported lead rather than a primary-source fact.
5. Retrieve the named college's session-specific affiliation order, including programme, campus and intake scope.
6. Where the one-time choice might apply, request the college election/trust resolution, MRSPTU affiliation order and IKGPTU acceptance/continuation order.
7. Resolve the student's admission cohort separately under the statutory transition provisions.
8. Retrieve the institution-specific AICTE Letter of Approval / Extension of Approval for the exact course, branch, intake and session, where AICTE approval applies.
9. Generate a claim-by-claim evidence report with source IDs, passages, effective dates, lineage, confidence/coverage state and the single most useful unresolved document request.

### Expected analyst-facing output

> **Historical affiliation transition detected — review required.** Punjab's legal instruments establish a 2015 territorial transition to MRSPTU and a 2018 one-time choice mechanism for eligible colleges. The college's present-day university cannot establish the correct awarding university for this student's historical cohort. Obtain the named college's 2017–18 affiliation order, its 2018 one-time choice and acceptance record, and the student's admission cohort or issuer award confirmation. Separately obtain the applicable 2018 AICTE approval record for the exact B.Tech branch.

This is a targeted evidence request, not a fraud verdict or candidate decision.

## 6. Programme approval remains an open gap

The AICTE handbook describes annual Extension of Approval and a process for change of affiliating university. In the reviewed 2017–18 handbook, the change process includes NOCs from the State Government, current affiliating university and proposed affiliating university, plus a trust/society/company resolution.

These requirements tell an analyst what to request. They do not establish that a named college obtained approval for a particular branch.

The IKGPTU 2019–20 AICTE college roster and aggregate course list are useful dated context, but neither is a substitute for the institution-specific 2017–18 or 2018–19 approval letter. The official 2018 course-wise college-list index was located, but its linked PDF did not retrieve in this pass.

**Next targeted retrieval:** select one named college from the 2018 roster and retrieve its AICTE 2017–18 and 2018–19 LoA/EoA plus the corresponding university course/affiliation order. Do not infer “not approved” from a failed search or missing web page.

## 7. Research significance

The pilot gives a concrete temporal-governance problem to measure:

- **Entity identity:** statutory name changes and aliases must not create false duplicate institutions.
- **Affiliation state:** territorial statute, implementation notification, election and accepted affiliation are separate events.
- **Cohort transition:** the awarding university can depend on admission date and statutory transition, not just current college affiliation.
- **Programme approval:** college affiliation and branch-specific approval are distinct evidence dimensions.
- **Authority and evidence type:** an Act defines legal powers; a Bill is only a proposal; a Registrar notice describes process; an accepted college-specific order proves the specific affiliation outcome.
- **Unknown versus contradiction:** a missing record is not proof of invalidity. The system must identify the smallest unresolved question and recommend the precise record or registrar confirmation needed.

The research gap is therefore a **claim-scoped, temporally versioned evidence resolution workflow** across statutes, affiliation records and programme approvals, with explicit source lineage and human escalation.

## 8. Limitations

1. The State Government notification implementing the MRSPTU Act's section 5(2) association date has not yet been captured.
2. No specific college's one-time choice application, acceptance or final affiliation order was retrieved.
3. No institution-specific AICTE LoA/EoA for a named 2018 B.Tech branch was retrieved.
4. The IKGPTU 2018 course-wise PDF was listed on the official index but could not be fetched.
5. PB-004's scan/date requires visual confirmation.
6. No evidence about unauthorised out-of-state study centres was collected in this pilot.
7. No individual student's roll number, admission record or degree award was checked.
8. No live connector, crawler, OCR pipeline, graph database or app feature was built or tested.

## 9. Decision and next research actions

**Decision: proceed with one more manual benchmark slice; do not begin national-scale ingestion or modify the app.**

The next slice should close the two strongest evidence gaps:

1. Obtain the State Government notification implementing MRSPTU's section 5(2) association date and one named college's 2017–18 affiliation / 2018 choice acceptance record.
2. Obtain a named college's AICTE LoA/EoA for a B.Tech branch across the 2017–19 sessions and the corresponding university course order.

Then ask a BGV analyst to review the report and measure whether it avoids a registrar contact, narrows the contact to one exact question, or simply shifts manual work elsewhere. Record analyst handling time, source retrieval time, number of unnecessary registrar follow-ups and the percentage of claims that can be resolved from accessible official evidence.

**Current status:** source feasibility is demonstrated for the statutory affiliation-transition pattern. College-specific affiliation and course-approval evidence remain incomplete. No application change is authorised by this pilot.
