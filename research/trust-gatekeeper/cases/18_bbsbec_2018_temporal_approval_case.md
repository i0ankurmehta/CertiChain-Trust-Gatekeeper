# 18 — Worked Historical Case: BBSBEC, Fatehgarh Sahib (2015–2019)

**Document ID:** TG-CASE-PB-001  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 15 November 2026  
**Status:** Desk-researched worked case; college-specific affiliation election and student award evidence remain open  
**Customer workflow:** Indian BGV analyst reviewing a scanned B.Tech degree  
**Institution:** Baba Banda Singh Bahadur Engineering College (BBSBEC), Fatehgarh Sahib, Punjab  
**Test programme:** B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (CSE), with Information Technology (IT) as a course-closure contrast  
**Application changes:** None. Manual research case and benchmark fixture only.

## 1. Why this is the first practical case

This college is a useful test because several different evidence types connect to one real institution and one historical period:

1. The Punjab statute explicitly places Fatehgarh Sahib within MRSPTU's territorial area.
2. MRSPTU's 2016 Board of Governors agenda records a State Government notification dated 30 June 2015 and says affected technical colleges became associated with MRSPTU from 1 July 2015.
3. IKGPTU's 2018–19 information brochure lists BBSBEC as an affiliated college and gives a college-specific programme list.
4. An AICTE Extension of Approval dated 10 April 2018 identifies IKGPTU as the affiliating body for BBSBEC in session 2018–19 and records approved intake by programme.
5. The same AICTE document marks Information Technology as a course applied for closure, with closure status approved, for session 2018–19.

This is more useful than simply searching whether a college exists. The records let us test a historical university transition, a positive programme-approval claim and a potentially inconsistent course/admission claim.

**Important:** these sources do not prove that a particular candidate attended BBSBEC or received a degree. No student record has been accessed.

## 3. Source evidence and supported claims

### EV-01 — MRSPTU territorial statute

**Source:** Maharaja Ranjit Singh State Technical University Act, 2014 (Punjab Act No. 5 of 2015), Punjab Government Gazette, 12 February 2015.  
**URL:** https://punjab.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/The-Maharaj-Ranjit-State-Technical-University-Act-2014.pdf

Section 5(1) explicitly includes Fatehgarh Sahib in MRSPTU's territorial area. Section 5(2) provides that technical colleges situated in the area would become associated with MRSPTU from a date to be notified by the State Government. It also contains a transition provision for students already studying for a PTU degree/diploma before that date.

**Supports:** Fatehgarh Sahib falls within MRSPTU's statutory territorial area; a statutory mechanism for college association and a student transition provision existed.

**Does not support:** actual affiliation of BBSBEC on a specific date, programme approvals at BBSBEC, or any student's roll/award.

### EV-02 — State implementation notification cited in MRSPTU Board material

**Source:** MRSPTU 5th Board of Governors agenda, meeting held 3 August 2016.  
**URL:** https://mrsptu.ac.in/images/Files/bos_agenda/Agenda_BOG_5.pdf

The agenda records State Government notification No. 7/33/2015-4TE2/5230/9/4, dated Chandigarh 30 June 2015, and states that technical colleges within MRSPTU's jurisdiction were deemed associated with MRSPTU with effect from 1 July 2015.

**Supports:** an official university Board document records the notification number/date and its stated effective date.

**Does not support:** the original State Government notification itself, or a BBSBEC-specific affiliation order. The original notification is still needed to close the primary-source gap.

### EV-03 — IKGPTU 2018–19 affiliated-college brochure

**Source:** IKGPTU Information Brochure 2018–19, Part E, “Affiliated Colleges of IKGPTU (Running AICTE Courses)”, entry 15.  
**URL:** https://ptu.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/22-6-18-Part-E-Affiliated-Colleges-of-IKGPTU_pdf.pdf

The brochure lists Baba Banda Singh Bahadur Engineering College, Fatehgarh Sahib, with B.Tech Agriculture Engineering (60), Civil Engineering (60), Computer Science & Engineering (180), Electrical Engineering (60), Electronics & Communication Engineering (60), and Mechanical Engineering (180). It also lists postgraduate programmes.

**Supports:** IKGPTU published BBSBEC in its 2018–19 affiliated-college brochure with a college-specific programme/intake listing.

**Does not support:** an individual student's admission, examination registration or degree award; nor does the brochure alone prove the exact date or legal mechanism of the college's affiliation change.

### EV-04 — AICTE Extension of Approval, 2018–19

**Source:** AICTE EoA, F.No. North-West/1-3517507353/2018/EOA, dated 10 April 2018, application 1-3517507353, permanent institute ID 1-14182101. The college-hosted PDF identifies AICTE as the issuing body.  
**URL:** https://bbsbec.edu.in/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/AICTE-EOA-Report_2018-19.pdf

The report identifies BBSBEC at Fatehgarh Sahib and names I.K. Gujral Punjab Technical University, Jalandhar, as the affiliating body for its listed courses in session 2018–19.

The report includes:
- B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering — approved intake 180;
- B.Tech Mechanical Engineering — approved intake 180;
- B.Tech Agriculture Engineering — approved intake 60;
- B.Tech Civil Engineering — approved intake 60;
- B.Tech Electrical Engineering — approved intake 60;
- B.Tech Electronics & Communication Engineering — approved intake 60.

It also lists Information Technology under “Course(s) Applied for Closure by the Institute for the Academic Year 2018–19” with “Course Closure Status Approved.”

**Supports:** an AICTE-generated EoA report records IKGPTU as the affiliating body for BBSBEC in session 2018–19; it records positive approval/intake for CSE and the other listed branches; and it records an approved closure status for IT for that session.

**Does not support:** the exact date BBSBEC changed affiliation, the content of its one-time choice application, the meaning of closure for a particular previously admitted student's teach-out rights, or an individual's degree authenticity.

**Source custody caveat:** the PDF is hosted on the college domain, although the document itself is an AICTE-generated report. For production use, independently retrieve or validate the same EoA through AICTE's official institution record/API or regulator archive.

### EV-05 — IKGPTU 2019–20 AICTE college list

**Source:** IKGPTU course-wise list of AICTE colleges for session 2019–20, dated 27 May 2019.  
**URL:** https://ptu.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/27-05-2019-Course-wise-List-of-AICTE-Colleges-of-IKGPTU-2019-20.pdf

BBSBEC appears in the private colleges section of the B.Tech list.

**Supports:** a later IKGPTU list also names BBSBEC among the B.Tech colleges for session 2019–20.

**Does not support:** 2018–19 branch-level approval or any individual's enrolment/award.

## 4. Reconstructed temporal graph

This is the minimum graph the research engine should be able to represent. Edge statements are scoped to the evidence that supports them.

| Date / interval | Entity or event | Relationship / claim | Evidence | Confidence / status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 Feb 2015 | MRSPTU | Statutorily established; territorial area includes Fatehgarh Sahib | EV-01 | Direct statutory evidence |
| 30 Jun 2015 | Punjab State Government notification 7/33/2015-4TE2/5230/9/4 | Notification cited as implementing association of colleges in MRSPTU territory | EV-02 | Referenced in official university Board agenda; original notification still needed |
| From 1 Jul 2015 | Technical colleges in Fatehgarh Sahib | Deemed associated with MRSPTU under the notification, as described in EV-02 | EV-01 + EV-02 | General territorial mechanism and reported implementation date; not a BBSBEC-specific order |
| 17 Jan 2018 | Punjab Act No. 4 of 2018 | One-time affiliation-choice mechanism introduced for eligible colleges; separate cohort provision | Source in pilot 17 | Statutory route exists; do not infer BBSBEC exercised it without its election/acceptance record |
| 10 Apr 2018 | BBSBEC, session 2018–19 | AICTE EoA records IKGPTU as affiliating body | EV-04 | Direct regulator-record claim for listed 2018–19 courses |
| 2018–19 | BBSBEC — B.Tech CSE | AICTE EoA lists approved intake 180 | EV-04; corroborated by EV-03 | Direct EoA course/intake evidence |
| 2018–19 | BBSBEC — B.Tech IT | AICTE EoA lists course closure as approved | EV-04 | Direct closure-status evidence; cohort impact requires interpretation/registrar confirmation |
| 2018–19 | BBSBEC | Listed in IKGPTU affiliated-college brochure | EV-03 | Direct university roster evidence |
| 2019–20 | BBSBEC | Included in IKGPTU course-wise AICTE college list | EV-05 | Later roster evidence |

### Graph modelling rules exposed by this case

- Fatehgarh Sahib being within MRSPTU's territorial area is not the same edge as BBSBEC being affiliated to MRSPTU.
- A statutory mechanism plus a general notification reference is not the same as a college-specific affiliation order.
- AICTE affiliating body = IKGPTU is a session-scoped claim; it must not be copied backward to every earlier academic year.
- AICTE course approval and course closure are separate time-scoped events.
- A course closure for a session must not automatically be interpreted as cancellation of degrees for students admitted in earlier sessions.
- A student-level award record is distinct from institution-level and programme-level evidence.

## 6. Analyst-facing report for BBS-CASE-C

> **Disposition: REVIEW REQUIRED — COURSE-SESSION CLOSURE SIGNAL**
>
> The institution is present in IKGPTU's 2018–19 affiliated-college brochure. AICTE's EoA dated 10 April 2018 records IKGPTU as the affiliating body for BBSBEC in session 2018–19. The same EoA lists B.Tech Information Technology as a course applied for closure, with closure status approved, for that academic session.
>
> The claimed admission to B.Tech Information Technology in 2018–19 therefore requires confirmation against the college's admission register and the applicable AICTE closure/teach-out record. The public evidence does not establish whether an exception or prior-cohort arrangement applies.
>
> **Do not conclude fraud or invalidate the degree from this evidence alone.**
>
> **Smallest unresolved question:** Was roll number SYN-BBS-2018-IT-001 actually admitted and registered in B.Tech Information Technology at BBSBEC for academic session 2018–19 under a valid admission/teach-out arrangement?

This is the intended product behaviour: show the source and precise unresolved fact, not a black-box risk score.

## 8. What this case proves — and what it does not

### Demonstrated by available records

- A statutory territorial transition affecting Fatehgarh Sahib existed.
- MRSPTU Board material records the relevant State notification number and 1 July 2015 effective date.
- IKGPTU's 2018–19 brochure names BBSBEC and lists its B.Tech courses and intakes.
- The 10 April 2018 AICTE EoA records IKGPTU as affiliating body for BBSBEC in 2018–19.
- The same EoA lists CSE as approved at 180 intake and IT as a course with approved closure status for 2018–19.

### Not demonstrated yet

- The original State Government notification dated 30 June 2015 (only referenced in the MRSPTU Board agenda in this evidence set).
- BBSBEC's one-time affiliation-choice application, trust resolution and IKGPTU acceptance/affiliation order.
- The precise effect of the IT closure on new admissions versus existing students or teach-out cohorts.
- Candidate R's admission register entry, roll number, examination registration, marksheets or degree-award register entry.
- Whether the scanned certificate is genuine.

## 9. Practical next actions

### Priority 1 — obtain the college-specific affiliation handoff record

Request from BBSBEC / IKGPTU:
- the college's one-time choice application or trust resolution under the 2018 amendment;
- the IKGPTU acceptance/final affiliation order and effective date;
- the corresponding MRSPTU 2017–18 affiliation/continuation record, if applicable.

This will turn the current session-level affiliating-body evidence into a better-supported institutional transition edge.

### Priority 2 — resolve IT course-closure semantics

Request from AICTE and/or IKGPTU:
- the detailed order/record supporting the approved IT closure for 2018–19;
- whether closure meant no new intake for that session, phase-out, or another status;
- any approved teach-out or continuation arrangements for students already admitted.

### Priority 3 — run the targeted analyst test

Give the synthetic BBS-CASE-A/B/C pack to one BGV analyst. Measure:
- time to reach a supported preliminary disposition;
- number of source documents opened;
- number of registrar questions required before and after the report;
- whether the report reduces the question to one exact cohort/programme issue;
- false escalation rate on the CSE positive controls.

Do not claim time/cost savings until measured with practitioners.
